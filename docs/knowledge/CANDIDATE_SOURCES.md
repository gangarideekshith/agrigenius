# Candidate knowledge sources — for owner approval (decision C3). Not ingested.

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method: web search and page fetches only; nothing was downloaded or ingested.
Research was cut short by a usage limit, and several publisher pages returned 403/404/timeouts. Anything marked UNCLEAR needs a human to open the page and read the licence before use.

Rating key:
- CONFIRMED = explicit licence statement found at the URL given in "Licence URL".
- CONFIRMED-NC = explicit licence found, but it is non-commercial. Usable only if the product stays non-commercial. Kept separate from the allowlist rows.
- UNCLEAR = no explicit licence statement found, or the page could not be read.
- NOT ALLOWED = licence found and it is not on the allowlist, or the terms are restrictive.

"Secondary" means the licence was read from a search-result summary, not from the publisher page itself. Re-check before relying on it.

## Notes

- Only 4 sources have a verified, allowlisted licence, and all are Wikimedia or COL. None is an Indian public institution, so Indian-institution coverage depends on written permission or per-document licence checks.
- GODL-India was not confirmed on any agronomy content. data.gov.in held only metadata for ICAR publications (page unreadable). Ask ICAR/DKMA whether the full-text PDFs are under GODL-India.
- Best Indic-language route: Vikaspedia (English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil and more). Its licence is the biggest open question. Recommend a written request to MeitY/C-DAC. Indic Wikipedias are licence-safe but sparse and variable in quality.
- Reuse of any CC BY-SA item means the derived text (chunks and translations) may carry share-alike obligations. Attribution must be shown in chatbot citations.
- Several licence readings came from search summaries because publisher pages returned 403 or timed out (FAO policy, Plantwise, data.gov.in, ICAR KRISHI, India Water Portal). Re-verify these directly.
- Not covered: sowing-season pages from the Ministry or ICAR with explicit licences, KVK collections, and agricultural university publications. These need another research pass.
- Nothing was downloaded or ingested. No other repository files were edited.
